# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Measure Me to be a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive project for everyone who participates.

Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting different opinions and approaches.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respecting people's privacy and personal boundaries.
- Helping create a friendly and collaborative environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately provocative comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Unwanted sexual or inappropriate behavior.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably make others feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Privacy

Measure Me deals with personal information such as body measurements and clothing sizes.

Contributors must not share another person's private or sensitive information without their explicit permission.

When reporting issues, avoid including real personal data whenever possible. Use fictional or anonymized examples instead.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

Please include enough information to understand what happened and, when possible, links or other relevant context.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related spaces.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in Measure Me, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful community.
